05 Before we begin

Good questions.
Clear starting points.

The details depend on your project. Here is what to clarify before either side commits.

Ask about your project →
How much will my project cost?

There is no fixed price list on this site. Share the type of project, the features or deliverables you need, and any budget range you have in mind. Scope and fees should be confirmed before work starts.

How long will it take?

Timing depends on the size of the project, the content and access available, and review requirements. Mention any target date in your enquiry so a suitable schedule can be discussed; no delivery date is promised by this website.

How do revisions work?

The number of review rounds, what counts as a revision, and how additional requests are handled need to be agreed for your project. Include any approval steps or stakeholders in your brief.

What are the payment terms?

Payment stages, any deposit, and accepted payment methods are confirmed for the individual project. This site does not collect payments or ask for card details.

What happens after delivery?

Handover, maintenance, updates, and ongoing support are separate points to clarify in the scope. Ask about access, source files, hosting, third-party subscriptions, and any support period before committing.

What should I send in my first message?

A few sentences are enough: what you are building, who will use it, the service you need, and any target date or budget. Links to references are useful. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information.

Can I see real client projects?

The showcase on this page contains labelled concept studies for fictional brands, not client commissions. Ask the studio what relevant work is available to review for your particular brief.

Enquiries & your privacyHow this page handles your details · Updated 12 November 2026

This notice describes the current enquiry flow on this website. It is not a promise about how every external service processes information.

What happens when you use the form

Your name, selected service, project description, and optional email address are used in your browser to prepare a WhatsApp message. This page has no form-submission backend and does not save those field values to browser storage. Submitting opens a wa.me link containing the message; those details are passed to WhatsApp as part of that link. You still need to send the message in WhatsApp. Use only information you are comfortable sharing there, as URLs may also appear in browser history.

After you contact the studio

If you send a message by WhatsApp or email, it becomes part of that conversation. Ask the studio directly about access to, correction of, or deletion of enquiry information, and about any retention arrangements. Do not include passwords, financial details, or other sensitive data in the form.

Browser storage

This page uses a small session-storage flag to remember that the opening animation has played in the current tab session. That flag contains no enquiry details. If browser storage is blocked, the site still works, but the opening may play again.

External services & hosting

The live page requests typefaces from Google Fonts. WhatsApp links and email links open external services with their own privacy practices. The hosting provider may process routine request data such as IP address, browser information, and access time; hosting log settings are not controlled by this HTML file. No analytics or advertising scripts are included in this page.
